Artist Biography

The Story Behind the Artist

J8-WISDM discovered rap in 2018 through "JESUS LA STAR" by Burundian artist El Georges. Inspired by the music, he began writing his own lyrics. In 2019, he performed for the first time at a Sunday Christian event. After a break around 2020–2021, he returned to music in 2022 with renewed motivation.

Performing in English, French, Kirundi and Swahili, J8-WISDM explores Drill, Trap, Dancehall, Afrobeat and Amapiano while seeking to communicate messages rooted in faith, integrity and purpose.
