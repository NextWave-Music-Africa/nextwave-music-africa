ABOUT THE ARTIST

Jonathan Wisdom, professionally known as J8-WISDM, is an independent artist whose creative journey combines music, personal expression, faith, and a vision for the next generation.

His musical journey began in 2018 after discovering Christian rap through the song "JESUS LA STAR" by El Georges. In 2019, he performed at a Christian event. After a period away from music, he returned to his artistic calling in 2022.

MUSIC & CREATIVITY

J8-WISDM explores different sounds and styles, including Christian rap, drill, trap, dancehall, Afrobeat, and Amapiano. His artistic expression also extends to video editing and digital visual creation.

FAITH & MISSION

His faith in Jesus Christ is central to his identity and purpose. Through music and creativity, he seeks to encourage young people to embrace faith, integrity, wisdom, responsibility, and a meaningful relationship with their Creator.

"My vision is to be a voice that inspires a generation to rediscover God, wisdom, and purpose."
