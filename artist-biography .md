## ABOUT THE ARTIST

NextWave Music Africa presents J8-WISDM, an emerging Burundian artist whose musical journey combines faith, creativity, and a vision to inspire the younger generation.

His journey into rap began in 2018 after discovering *JESUS LA STAR*, a song by Burundian artist El Georges. Inspired by the music, he began writing his own lyrics and gradually developing his artistic identity.

In 2019, he experienced his first live performance at a Sunday Christian event. After stepping away from music around 2020–2021, he returned in 2022 with renewed motivation and a clearer artistic direction.

J8-WISDM expresses himself through four languages: English, French, Kirundi, and Swahili. His musical universe includes Drill, Trap, Dancehall, Afrobeat, and Amapiano.

## MUSIC WITH A PURPOSE

As a believer in Jesus Christ, J8-WISDM sees music as more than entertainment. His artistic vision is to glorify Jesus Christ, encourage young people to seek God, and inspire a generation to embrace integrity, strong values, and a meaningful purpose in life.

Through music, public speaking, and creative expression, he aspires to become a positive voice for young people.

## THE VISION

J8-WISDM's journey continues to evolve, with ambitions that extend beyond music into youth inspiration, creativity, and meaningful social influence.

NextWave Music Africa highlights his journey as an emerging Burundian talent whose artistic identity combines contemporary sounds with faith and purpose.

His story is still being written, and his vision reaches far beyond music itself.
